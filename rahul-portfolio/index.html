About

I'm a B.Sc Computer Science student who learns best by building. I'm interested in creating useful software and practical digital solutions, and I'd rather work through a real problem than just study it in theory.

Outside coursework, I founded and help run Bikergram Andrapradesh, a motorcycle community connecting Bikers across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana — an experience that taught me a lot about coordinating people and solving problems on the ground. I bring that same practical approach into the projects I'm currently developing: the Bikergram Website, Urban Tray, and the MeeSeva Dashboard.
